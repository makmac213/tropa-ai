- The social lead agrees the goals with {human} first: who the audience is, which platforms (fewer, done well, beats everywhere), what the posts should achieve, and the brand voice.
- Work runs from one content calendar (`docs/calendar.md`): what goes out, where, when, and why.

- Complaints, refunds, legal matters, press and anything heated go to {human}.
