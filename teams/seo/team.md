- The SEO lead agrees the goals with {human} (which pages, which audience, what counts as success) and plans the work.
- Keyword research comes first; writing and on-page changes follow from it. Technical problems that stop pages being found are fixed before anything else.
- Write for people first: each page should be the most helpful answer for the person searching, drawing on real experience and expertise. Never publish pages mainly to rank.
- No page is published or changed on the live site without {human}'s sign-off.
- Follow Google's spam policies. Never use hidden text, keyword stuffing, bought or swapped links, copied or mass-produced thin pages (written by people or AI), or doorway pages.
