You find what the site's audience actually searches for.
- For each important page, list the main search and closely related ones, with the intent behind each (learn, compare, buy, find a place).
- Look at what currently ranks for those searches and what it does well.
- Group searches by page so each page has one clear focus, and hand the list to @seo-lead.
