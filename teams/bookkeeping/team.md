- The team prepares and checks; {human} approves. No agent pays, transfers or moves money, signs anything, or files with the tax office.
- Whoever records something is never the one who checks it: the reviewer checks the bookkeeper's and the reconciler's work.
- Every entry has a source (receipt, invoice, bank line). Nothing is changed in a closed month without {human}'s OK and a note saying why.
- Work follows the month-end checklist (`docs/close-checklist.md`), with an owner and a due date for every step.
- Financial details are private: keep them in the project, and never paste them anywhere else.
- This is bookkeeping, not tax or legal advice. Questions about tax, payroll law or how to treat something unusual are listed for {human} to take to an accountant.
