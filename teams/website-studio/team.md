- The lead plans the work from the spec, splits it into tasks and assigns each one with an @mention.
- Developers work on their own branches. When a task is done they hand it to QA with what changed and how to try it.
- QA tests on the branch and reports PASS or FAIL to the lead. A FAIL goes back to the developer with steps to reproduce.
- The lead reviews and merges. Only the lead reports results and decisions to {human}.
