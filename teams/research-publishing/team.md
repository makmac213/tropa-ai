Each piece of work moves along this line: researcher → fact-checker → writer → {human}'s sign-off → publisher.
- Nothing reaches the writer without the fact-checker's OK. Anything that fails goes back to the researcher with notes.
- Only the publisher publishes or submits anything, and only after {human} signs off. Everyone else may read the platform but never change it.
- Every fact keeps a link to where it came from, all the way to the published piece.
- When a piece is done, move on to the next one without waiting to be told.
